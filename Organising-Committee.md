His research combines observations of wild animals with cognitive experiments in captive populations.</p>

    <div class="post-card__inner committee-card__inner">
      <div class="post-card__header">
        <h2>Teresa Romero</h2>
      </div>
      <p>Teresa's research focuses on the social and emotional skills that animals employ when making decisions about when, and with whom, to interact. She takes a comparative and interdisciplinary approach, integrating theoretical frameworks and methodological tools from natural sciences (e.g., animal behaviour, physiology, chemistry) and social sciences (psychology, art-based research) to test theory-driven hypotheses. This research interest allows her to explore aspects of sociality, conflict resolution, cooperation, and empathy-driven behaviours across a range of taxa, including carnivores, primates and rodents. She is interested in applying fundamental knowledge in animal social complexity to the welfare of both captive and wild animals through interdisciplinary research and the use of emerging technologies.</p>
    </div>
