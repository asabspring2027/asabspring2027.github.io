## Abstract submission: opens on TBC - 15th February 2027

Your work can be presented at ASAB Spring 2027 in one of these two formats:
<ul>
    <li>
    Standard talk
  </li>
  <li>
    Poster
  </li>
</ul>

### Selection process
Oral or poster presentations will be selected based on the submitted title (12 words maximum) and abstract (200 words maximum). Abstracts should be clear, concise and consist of:
<ul>
    <li>
    Brief background
  </li>
  <li>
    A clear question / aim
  </li>
  <li>
    Brief methodology
 </li>
  <li>
    Overall results
  </li>
  <li>
      Conclusions / significance
  </li>
</ul>

Please note that while we have limited space for standard talks, rejected talk submissions might be offered a poster slot. Please indicate in the registration form if you would like to be considered for a poster if not successful for an oral presentation.

We welcome speakers to deliver their talks in the language with which they are most comfortable, but we ask that your abstract and the text on your slides be in English. The deadline for abstract submissions is <strong>February 15th 2027<strong>.

### Standard Talks
Talks should be 12 minutes long, with 3 minutes for questions and discussion (each oral presentation slot is 15 minutes).

### Posters
Posters should be A1 (59.4 cm x 84.1 cm), portrait orientation. The poster sessions will take place on Conference Days 1 and 2. 

### Research Ethics
Presenters/authors must declare that all work has been carried out in accordance with ASAB’s Guidelines for the Treatment of Animals in Research. We will not consider for presentation any research that failed to adhere to the highest standards of animal welfare, and may contact authors for clarification before considering a submission. There is a space on the registration form clarifying that your work adhered to all ethical standards. We are also not accepting work produced by generative artificial intelligence. All submissions must represent original research conducted by the authors.
